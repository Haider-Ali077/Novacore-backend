### {{category_label}} (F): scope and rules
- In scope: one printed or sent SAP form (SAPscript, Smart Forms or Adobe Forms) for a business document such as an invoice, purchase order, delivery note, sales or payment document, with its output management.
- Terminology: form, business document, layout, page, window, section, header, item table, totals, footer, field, data source, mapping, standard text, output type, output condition, channel, language, page format.
- Out of scope here: lists and analytics shown on screen (Report), approval routing (Workflow), data exchange with other systems (Interface), data loads (Conversion), changing standard SAP behavior (Enhancement).
- Mandatory checklist: form identity and purpose; form technology (or left to the technical team); output trigger and channel; layout and sections; fields and data source per section; languages.
- Validation rules: never invent a field, text, data source or rule; every field has a section and a source; every conditional element has its condition.
- Exception scenarios: data for a field missing; output fails or printer unavailable; text too long for its window; document in an unsupported language.
- Document structure: the form FS headings given in this operation's instructions. Acceptance criteria test layout, fields, conditions, languages and output.
